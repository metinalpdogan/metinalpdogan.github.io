Robot learning · Human-in-the-loop learning · Reliable embodied AI

Metin Alp Dogan

I'm a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UCLA, where I'm fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yuchen Cui in the Robot Intelligence Lab. My research interests lie at the intersection of robot learning, human-in-the-loop learning, and safe and reliable embodied AI. I'm particularly interested in developing robot policies that can learn from human feedback, be interpretable and steerable, and operate reliably under real-world uncertainty.

I was selected as a Fulbright Scholar by the Fulbright Commission and subsequently completed my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA. Before joining UCLA, I was a research intern at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, where I worked with Prof. Metin Sitti on untethered magnetic milli-robots for biomedical applications. My research experiences have spanned both the physical design and control of robotic systems and the development of learning-based approaches to robot intelligence, shaping my interest in building robots that are not only capable, but also reliable and trustworthy.

Projects

Conformer Decoder for Intracortical Speech BCIs

Adapted the ASR Conformer to decode phonemes from 256-channel intracortical recordings (Brain-to-Text ’24), adding day-specific input layers, SpecAugment and intermediate CTC. Phoneme error rate 0.161 vs. 0.217 for the GRU baseline, with no language model.

EVEREST: Decoding Keystrokes from Wrist sEMG

TDS convolutions for short muscle-activation patterns feeding a three-layer LSTM, plus amplitude scaling and tuned SpecAugment, on Meta’s emg2qwerty. Test character error rate 13.29 vs. 21.91 for the baseline.

move = goal · click = start

Artificial Potential Function Navigation for a TurtleBot

Robot-centric navigation function U = γk/β: an attractive goal term over a product of obstacle barrier terms, with Kinect RGB-D colour segmentation locating the target and obstacles. Its normalized gradient steers the TurtleBot in ROS and Gazebo. The thumbnail runs the same field live.
